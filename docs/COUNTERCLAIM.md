# Counterclaim against Multiplicity

**Claimant.** Bel esprit D'accord Trust, by SnapKitty Research Lab.
**Researchers.** Ahmad Ali Parr and Jessica Westerhoff.
**Respondent work.** Ty Van Gelder, published byline Ryan O. Van Gelder, Multiplicity Foundation / Citizen Gardens. *Multiplicity Theory — Foundations and Applications*, DOI 10.13140/RG.2.2.18053.46568, July 2025. *QMHES — Mathematical Appendix*, Multiplicity Foundation, 2026, PhaseMirror-HQ.
**Date.** 8 October 2026.
**Instrument.** Research counterclaim. Not a court pleading.

## Claim countered

Van Gelder claims that prime-indexed multiplicity is a cryptographic operator: a matrix \(\mathcal{M}_t\) and coupling tensor \(\mathcal{T}_t\) bound into AEAD associated data and into HKDF, with geometric convergence under equation 24, and that no prior system combines his four layers. He also claims, in the 2025 foundations preprint, that prime-powered multiplicities increase the complexity of factoring enough to resist Shor's algorithm.

## Counterclaim

1. Equation 24 is gradient ascent. Proposition 3.13 states a descent theorem. The implication does not hold.
2. Definition 3.7 appends \(\arg(\alpha_j)\). Unitarity does not put that vector on the unit sphere.
3. SVD clipping is not a distributed consensus protocol. Distinct encodings desynchronize his own Definition 3.12.
4. Table 1 of the appendix marks the multiplicity layer "formally unproven" and the frequency layer "hardness TBD." A layer so marked is not a security theorem.
5. Prime labels on a message do not change the order of Shor's algorithm on an integer. Unique factorization is the structure Shor exploits, not a defense against it.
6. ADR 004 of this repository removes \(\mathcal{M}_t\), \(\mathcal{T}_t\), and \(f\) from the normative path. Encapsulation, signatures, AEAD, and the transcript hash remain the FIPS and RFC constructions he already cited. Those constructions do not need Multiplicity Theory.

## Relief in this repository

The multiplicity operator is withdrawn. ADR 001–003 are retained as the abandonment record and are superseded by [ADR 004](../adr/ADR-004.md).
