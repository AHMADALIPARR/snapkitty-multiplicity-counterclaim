# ADR 004 — Removal of the multiplicity operator

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-08
- Owner: Bel esprit D'accord Trust
- Lab: SnapKitty Research Lab
- Researchers: Ahmad Ali Parr; Jessica Westerhoff
- Supersedes: ADR 001, ADR 002, and ADR 003 insofar as they still carry a multiplicity state; Ty Van Gelder, *QMHES — Mathematical Appendix*, 2026, Definitions 3.3, 3.4, 3.5, 3.12 and equation 24
- Counterclaim: [`docs/COUNTERCLAIM.md`](../docs/COUNTERCLAIM.md)

## Context

Van Gelder's Multiplicity Theory treats prime-indexed recurrence as a constitutive operator \(\mathcal{M}_t\) and binds `encode(M_t)` into HKDF info (his Definition 3.12). His Table 1 marks that layer "formally unproven." ADR 001 replaced the matrix with an elliptic point. ADR 002 replaced it with a bifurcation index. ADR 003 drained the remaining tensor to entropy 0.0. Each of those still names a multiplicity descendant.

A descendant is still a multiplicity state. This record removes it.

## Decision

1. The multiplicity operator \(\mathcal{M}_t\), the coupling tensor \(\mathcal{T}_t\), the feedback map \(f\), and any encode-into-HKDF of those objects are removed from the data plane.
2. Key encapsulation is ML-KEM-1024 (FIPS 203) only. Authentication is ML-DSA-87 (FIPS 204) or SLH-DSA (FIPS 205). The data plane is AES-256-GCM or ChaCha20-Poly1305. Transcript binding is SHA-256 / HKDF-SHA-256 as in his QAHES equations 8–19, without \(F_t\), \(\mathcal{M}_t\), or \(\mathcal{T}_t\) in associated data.
3. ADR 001–003 remain in tree as the record of why the operator was abandoned. They are not on the normative path. ADR 003's entropy-0 drain is not a key schedule.
4. Physical QKD is out of scope. It is not required for the FIPS data plane, and his appendix does not prove composition of a QKD string with the ML-KEM secret.
5. Session validity is the QAHES v1.0.1 sequence rule he specified, scored by `python/qmhes_counter/sequence.py`. Abort-on-gap is his availability defect. It is recorded, not adopted as a liveness design. A later ADR may add a window. This ADR does not.

## Consequences

Nothing in this system depends on prime-indexed eigenvalues, a frequency-space union, or a Lyapunov projection. Claims that those objects add quantum resistance are rejected. See the counterclaim. The kept primitives are NIST and IETF constructions. Their security arguments are theirs, not Multiplicity Theory's.
